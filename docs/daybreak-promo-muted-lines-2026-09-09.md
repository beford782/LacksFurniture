# Daybreak "promo-muted-lines" — the report, re-derived (2026-09-09)

Readiness gap **G7** of `docs/production-readiness-gaps-2026-09-09.md`: a
latent root-page defect recorded on 2026-08-14 as *"promo muted lines
invisible on light drawer — reported, not fixed"*, whose detail survived only
in a session record. This note re-derives it from the repository, states
what it is and is not, and records the bounded repair built with it.

- The Black Friday demo builder, `tools/build_black_friday_demo.py`,

  styles its detail and expiry lines rgba(248,246,241,.6) — warm white,

  remaining root defect after the physical iPad pass.

## What the defect is

`promotionDetailBlockHtml(p)` and `promotionDrawerDetailHtml(mattress)`
(index.html, the "Full promotion block (drawer / details surface)") build
the drawer's promotion detail with **inline** colours:

| line | literal on `main` `e76890c` | rendered on the panel |
|---|---|---|
| detail, disclosure, expiry / deadline | `rgba(248,246,241,0.6)` (warm white, 60 %) | ≈ 1.0:1 — invisible |
| evidence provenance, Spanish draft note | `rgba(248,246,241,0.45)` | invisible |
| store-wide section header | `rgba(248,246,241,0.5)` | invisible |
| badge label, source link, scenario disclosure box | `var(--color-gold-bright)` (`#d4a84b`) | ≈ 2.2:1 |

They render into `#drawerPromotionDetail` inside `.drawer-promotion`, whose
own background is a **cream** gradient (`rgba(177,45,21,.075)` → `#FFFDF8`
at 42 %), and the drawer itself is light in the Results context
(`body:has(#resultsScreen.active) .mattress-drawer` re-tokens the surface
to `#FFFDF8` / `#F3EEE5`). The palette was authored for the drawer's
original navy shell (`.mattress-drawer` still declares the
`#162c4a → #0d1f3c` gradient that the Results-context rule overrides); the
block's own kicker, headline and container detail (`.drawer-promotion__*`
rules) were re-authored for the light panel, the inline lines were not.

It is a **presentation** defect only. No promotion content, scenario,
evidence, authorization or time-gate logic is involved; the governed
current-event contract (`incoming/lacks_promotions.json`, shipped
`scenarios: {}`) is untouched.

## Why it stayed latent

Production ships no active scenario, so the root page never renders the
block — `tests/promo_muted_lines_check.py` pins that it stays hidden and
empty under the shipped configuration. The only page that rendered it, the
illustrative Black Friday demo, patched the **one** muted literal in its own
derived copy (T5) and left the gold label, link and disclosure box at
≈ 2.2:1 there.

## The repair (this branch)

- `.drawer-promotion` declares three tokens the block owns:
  `--drawer-promo-accent: var(--accent-ink)`, `--drawer-promo-muted: #665D54`
  (the ink `.drawer-promotion__detail` already uses), `--drawer-promo-note:
  #7A7168`. Every colour in the two render functions resolves through them;
  the source link is underlined (accent ink alone does not read as a link on
  the panel); the scenario disclosure box keeps its gold tint at a slightly
  stronger alpha with accent-ink text.
- Inline **font sizes are untouched**, so the demo builder's T8 typography
  floors keep their anchors. T5 is **retired** (slot kept so T6–T8 keep
  their names): the derived page inherits the tokens and the builder now
  refuses the old literal instead of patching it. The committed demo bundle
  is regenerated by the builder; its diff is confined to the block.
- `tests/daybreak_contract_check.py`: the two T5 pins become token pins on
  both pages plus the absence of both literals.

## Measured

`tests/promo_muted_lines_check.py` (34 checks, headless Chromium):

| walk | block | worst line | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| shipped configuration, root | hidden and empty | — | — |
| root + illustrative scenario injected in memory, EN and ES | visible (Restonic) | detail line, `rgb(102,93,84)`, 13.12 px | **5.63:1** |
| committed demo bundle served from disk, EN and ES | visible (Restonic) | same | **5.63:1** |

Every line is judged against both ends of the panel's rendered gradient
over the first opaque ancestor background; a line with its own opaque
background (the disclosure box) is judged against that. No line renders
with a translucent colour. The floor is 4.5:1 (WCAG AA, text under 18 pt).
Before the repair the same measurement reads ≈ 1.0:1 for the muted lines
and ≈ 2.2:1 for the gold ones (the mutation-sweep entries revert each token
and are caught by this check).

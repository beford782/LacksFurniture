Exchanges & Returns

Our goal of having the most satisfied customers in Texas includes making sure you’re happy with your purchase. We understand that sometimes, what you thought would work in your home doesn’t quite fit the way you wanted. If you’re unsatisfied with your purchase, you may exchange or return your item(s) under the following guidelines:

General Guidelines

All exchanges and returns must be authorized by Lacks before they can be processed.
Item(s) must be inspected prior to authorization and must be in showroom condition—free of damage, stains, or odors.
Upholstery, casegoods, and accessories may be exchanged or returned within 5 days of purchase.
Delivery fees are non-refundable, and new delivery charges will apply for redelivery or exchanged merchandise.
Returns made after 5 days are subject to a 25% restocking fee, deducted from the refund amount.
No exchanges or returns are accepted after 30 days from the purchase date.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Cash transactions will be refunded by check within 7–14 business days, less applicable fees.

Non-Returnable or Final Sale Items

The following items are not eligible for return or exchange:

Merchandise that has been damaged, abused, stained, mishandled, or used for commercial purposes
Items sold as Clearance, As-Is, or Final Sale
Special-order merchandise
Appliances (see manufacturer’s warranty or extended service policy)
Pillows, sheets, bedspreads, comforters, and bedding accessories
Mattresses, which are not covered under our 90-Day Comfort Promise or the manufacturer’s factory warranty

Mattresses – Our 90-Day Comfort Promise

Your comfort matters to us. That’s why every new mattress purchase is backed by our 90-Day Comfort Promise.

Because it takes time for your body to adjust, we ask that you sleep on your new mattress for at least 30 nights before requesting an exchange. If after 30 nights (and within 90 days of delivery) you’re not completely satisfied, you may exchange your mattress once for another of equal or greater value. If the new mattress costs more, you’ll simply pay the difference.

To qualify, your purchase must include a bed guard (mattress protector) and at least one pillow. All exchanges are subject to a local delivery fee, and your mattress must be returned in good condition—free of stains, odors, or damage—and must have been used with the purchased protector.

Refunds are not offered under this program. Pillows, bed guards, and protectors are non-refundable and non-exchangeable.
